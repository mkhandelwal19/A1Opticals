Returns and refunds

Preview build. These are the terms as we understand them from a first conversation — every line here is confirmed with A1 Opticals before anything goes live, and the payment gateway will not activate without them being real.

Cancelling an order

Any order can be cancelled free of charge before it goes into the lab or the van — call or WhatsApp with the order reference. Your prescription can be changed free within 24 hours of ordering. Once lenses have been cut to your power, the order can still be returned under the 14-day policy below.

14-day returns on glasses

Eyeglasses, sunglasses, reading glasses and kids’ frames can be returned within fourteen days of delivery for a full refund or an exchange — including pairs with powered lenses, because a pair you cannot see through is our problem to fix. Bring them to the store or ask us to collect. Refunds go back to the original payment method within five working days of receipt.

Contact lenses and accessories

Unopened boxes within fourteen days. Opened contact lens boxes cannot be returned.

Trade returns

Unopened frames, sunglasses, stock lenses and contact lenses can be returned within seven days of delivery for credit against your account. Cut or edged lenses and anything made to a prescription supplied per line cannot be returned.

Warranty

  • Frames — twelve months against manufacturing defects in the hinge, plating and acetate. Breakage in use is not covered.
  • Lenses — coating defects replaced within six months. Scratches are not a coating defect.
  • Instruments — twelve months parts and labour, serviced on site in the Tricity.

How a return works

  1. Raise it from your account, or WhatsApp the order reference.
  2. Bring it to the store, we collect it with the van, or we send a courier label.
  3. Refund within five working days of receipt; trade credit shows on your next invoice.

Faulty or wrong items

Our mistake, our cost. Replaced on the next dispatch, with the return collected at the same time. No seven-day limit applies to a genuine fault.
