Privacy policy

Preview build. These policies are written as real policies rather than placeholder text because the payment gateway will not activate a live account without them, and because a customer who reads one deserves a straight answer. Every line is confirmed with A1 Opticals before anything goes live.

What we collect

WhatWhy
Name, phone, emailTo confirm the order and send the invoice
Delivery addressTo get the parcel to you
Order historySo you can re-order and we can handle returns
Your prescriptionTo cut your lenses, and so you need not type it again
Bag contentsKept in your browser so it survives a reload

What we do not collect

Card numbers. We never see them. Payment details go straight to Razorpay, who are PCI-DSS certified for exactly this reason. What comes back to us is a payment identifier and a yes or no.

Who else touches your data

  • Razorpay — payment processing. They receive your name, email and phone so they can send a receipt and handle chargebacks.
  • The courier — name, address and phone. They cannot deliver without them.
  • Supabase — our database host. Data is stored in their Mumbai region.
  • Zoho — sends the confirmation and invoice emails.

That is the entire list. We do not sell your data, we do not rent it, and we do not hand it to advertisers.

Cookies and local storage

Your bag, saved address and prescription live in your browser's local storage, not in a cookie sent to us. A session cookie keeps you logged in. We do not run advertising or cross-site tracking, so there is no consent banner here — there is nothing to consent to.

How long we keep it

Order records for eight years, because Indian tax law requires it. Everything else until you ask us to delete it.

Your rights

Write to us and you can ask for a copy of what we hold, a correction, or deletion. We will do it within 30 days. Deletion cannot cover invoices we are legally required to keep, and we will say so plainly rather than pretend otherwise.

Security

Traffic is encrypted in transit. Database access is restricted by row-level security so one customer cannot read another's orders. Payment verification happens on a server, never in your browser, because anything a browser asserts a browser can be made to lie about.

Children

This shop is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their data.
