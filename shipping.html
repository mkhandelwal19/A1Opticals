Delivery policy

Preview build. These are the terms as we understand them from a first conversation — every number here is confirmed with A1 Opticals before anything goes live, and the payment gateway will not activate without them being real.

Where we deliver

Everywhere in India. Inside Chandigarh, Mohali, Panchkula, Zirakpur and Kharar our own van brings it, in the slot you choose at checkout. Beyond that, a tracked courier. You can also collect from the store at no charge. Trade orders for opticians go by the van only; instruments are delivered and installed by our own engineer within the Tricity, and couriered with a video walkthrough elsewhere.

Dispatch time

Glasses with power are fitted in our own lab, usually within 48 hours of receiving your prescription, and nothing is cut until we have it. Frames without power, sunglasses, readers, contact lenses and accessories dispatch the same day if ordered before 2 pm on a working day, otherwise the next working day.

  • Progressive and thin lenses — three to four working days from the lab.
  • Rx lenses — made to prescription, three to four working days from the lab, then couriered.
  • Non-stock contact lens powers — five to seven working days.
  • Instruments — installation is booked with you by phone after the order is confirmed.

Charges

Free delivery on orders above ₹999; below that, ₹99 by van or courier. Collection from the store is always free. Trade orders: free in the Tricity above ₹10,000, otherwise ₹250 per consignment. Every charge is shown on the order before you confirm it, and the owner can change the thresholds from the dashboard.

Damaged in transit

Check the parcel before signing. Report transit damage with a photograph within 48 hours and it is replaced on the next dispatch at our cost.
